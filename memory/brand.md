# Brand

Fill this in before the first piece. Every seat reads it; the lead owns it.

## Who we are talking to

- One paragraph: who they are, what they already know, what they are trying to do.

## How we sound

- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No hype, no exclamation marks.
- Say the thing, then the caveat. Never the caveat first.

## Words we use and words we avoid

- Use: _<three to five words your audience says>_.
- Avoid: _<the words that make you sound like everyone else>_.

## Locations and spellings

- Pick one: American or British spelling. One spelling of each product name.

## What we never do

- Invent a customer, a quote, a metric or a partnership.
- Promise a date we have not checked.
