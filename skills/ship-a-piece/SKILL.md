# Ship a piece

1. Read `memory/brand.md` and the last three published pieces.
2. Draft to the acceptance line: length, audience, one call to action.
3. Cut a tenth. Read it out loud once; fix what you stumbled on.
4. Check every claim: product claims against the repo, numbers against a named report and date.
   Cut anything you cannot check.
5. Write the evidence line: where the draft lives, its word count, and what you checked.
6. Hand it to the lead. The lead publishes and closes the line.
